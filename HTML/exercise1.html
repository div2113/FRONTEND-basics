The Amazing World of Chemistry

Chemistry is the science that studies matter and how different substances interact with one another. Everything around us, from water to air and even our own bodies, is made of tiny particles called atoms. Chemistry helps us understand natural processes and develop products that improve our everyday lives.

What is an Atom?

An atom is the smallest unit of an element that retains its chemical properties.
Every atom is made up of three main particles:

1.Protons (positive charge)
2.Neutrons (no charge)
3.Electrons (negative charge)

Water – The Most Important Compound

Water is one of the most essential substances on Earth. It covers nearly 71% of the Earth's surface and is necessary for all living organisms. A water molecule consists of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.

Chemical Formula:
H2O

Chemical Reactions and Formulas

A chemical reaction occurs when substances combine to form new substances. These reactions are represented using chemical equations. Scientists also use mathematical formulas in chemistry to calculate area, volume, and density.


Formation of Water:
2H2 + O2 = 2H2O

Sulfuric Acid
2H+ + SO42- = H2SO4

Another Formula:
Ca2 + CO32- = CaCO3
